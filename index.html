Pricing Transparency

Good, privacy-aware AI services are expensive to run. We keep pricing honest and explain where each euro goes.

45%

Speech-to-text, summarization, and translation model usage.

Cloud infrastructure

25%

Hosting, storage, logs, monitoring, and reliability costs.

Payment processing

15%

Stripe / SumUp transaction fees, charge handling, and payouts.

Operations & GDPR

15%

Support, compliance work, legal documentation, and maintenance.

Example: A 0.99 EUR package is not pure margin. A significant part directly covers model, cloud, and payment costs.

This cost split is an operational estimate and can vary by traffic, provider prices, and feature usage.
